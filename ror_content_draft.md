## Design Rules Summary

# ROR Design Rules for Content Packs

Source: Gorilla Studio identity direction and `colors_and_type.css`.

## Core Visual Language

Rock On Ruby should feel bold, giftable, cheeky and useful. The brand does not need soft lifestyle styling to be warm.

Default design traits:

- Beige or cream canvas
- Black ink
- Hard black outlines
- Flat colour blocks
- Offset block shadows
- Big type
- Strong product close-ups
- One or two loud accents, not a muddy rainbow
- Sharp, practical layouts that feel shoppable

Avoid:

- Generic pastel Instagram templates
- Overly soft script-heavy styling
- Corporate minimalism
- Blurry mood imagery
- Stock-photo energy
- Designs that make the product hard to inspect

## Type Direction

Use the design system as a voice selector:

- Druk Wide: big ROR statements, campaign headers, poster moments.
- Druk: tall condensed editorial display, bold hooks, attitude-led content.
- Sharp Grotesk: everyday headings and body copy.
- Cooper Black: retro, warm, funny, softer nostalgia.
- Prohibition or Futura Condensed: patch, badge, pub, dad, sport and poster energy.
- Old English: varsity, heritage, year tops and bold personalised moments.
- ITC Garamond: quieter story-led editorial moments.

## Palette Direction by Moment

### Father's Day

Use beige, black, navy, army green, orange and dark teal.

Feel:

- Practical
- Bold
- Giftable
- A bit dry and funny
- Less pink-led unless the product itself needs it

Good for:

- Caps
- Slogan sweatshirts
- BBQ or food angles
- Dad gift guides
- Last-minute gifting

### Mother's Day and Gifts for Mum

Use beige, cream, black, pink, bright red, baby pink and butter.

Feel:

- Warm
- Personal
- Thoughtful
- Not twee
- Still a bit funny

Good for:

- Personalised sweatshirts
- Tote bags
- Make-up bags
- Gift guides
- Story-led emails

### Valentine's and Galentine's

Use beige, black, bright red, pink, hot pink and baby pink.

Feel:

- More mate-to-mate than romantic cliche
- Anti-boring

Good for:

- Personalised accessories

### Birthday and Milestone Gifting

Use beige, black, bright red, pink, navy, burgundy, gold and Old English or varsity type where useful.

Feel:

- Clear gift intent

Use cream, black, bright red, dark red, forest green, army green and gold.

Feel:
