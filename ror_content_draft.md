# ROR Design Rules for Content Packs

Source: Gorilla Studio identity direction and `colors_and_type.css`.

## Core Visual Language

Rock On Ruby should feel bold, giftable, cheeky and useful. The brand does not need soft lifestyle styling to be warm.

Default design traits:

- Beige or cream canvas
- Black ink
- Hard black outlines
- Flat colour blocks
- Offset block shadows
- Big type
- Strong product close-ups
- One or two loud accents, not a muddy rainbow
- Sharp, practical layouts that feel shoppable

Avoid:

- Generic pastel Instagram templates
- Overly soft script-heavy styling
- Corporate minimalism
- Blurry mood imagery
- Stock-photo energy
- Designs that make the product hard to inspect

## Type Direction

Use the design system as a voice selector:

- Druk Wide: big ROR statements, campaign headers, poster moments.
- Druk: tall condensed editorial display, bold hooks, attitude-led content.
- Sharp Grotesk: everyday headings and body copy.
- Cooper Black: retro, warm, funny, softer nostalgia.
- Prohibition or Futura Condensed: patch, badge, pub, dad, sport and poster energy.
- Old English: varsity, heritage, year tops and bold personalised moments.
- ITC Garamond: quieter story-led editorial moments.
